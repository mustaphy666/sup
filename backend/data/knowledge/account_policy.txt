SUPPORTFLOW ACCOUNT POLICY

Customers can contact SupportFlow regarding account, subscription, payment, and transaction issues.

SupportFlow verifies customer information before accessing account-specific information.

SupportFlow protects customer information and does not disclose private account information to other customers.

If an issue cannot be resolved using the available support tools, SupportFlow may create a support ticket for human assistance.

Support tickets are assigned a unique ticket ID and initially have an open status.

Customers should never provide passwords, one-time passwords, full payment card numbers, or other sensitive authentication information through support conversations.
