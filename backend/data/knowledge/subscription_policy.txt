SUPPORTFLOW REFUND POLICY

Customers may request a refund for eligible completed transactions.

Refund requests are reviewed after submission.

Duplicate charges caused by an accidental repeated payment may be eligible for a refund.

Refund requests normally remain pending until they are reviewed and processed.

A customer should not assume that a refund has been completed simply because a refund request was submitted.

Customers should provide the relevant transaction information when requesting assistance with a duplicate charge.

If a transaction is not eligible for a refund, the customer may contact SupportFlow support for further assistance.

SupportFlow does not guarantee that every refund request will be approved.
